What the application provides

TubeMilestones reads data that you authorize, stores current and historical progress in its cloud architecture, and presents milestones, simple Analytics, and user-created goals. Its requested YouTube permissions are read-only; it does not upload, edit, delete, or manage YouTube content.

Accounts and authorization

You are responsible for access to your TubeMilestones account and any Google accounts you connect. TubeMilestones login may use email/password or Google; application login and YouTube authorization are separate, and a connected YouTube account may differ from the login identity. You may connect multiple YouTube accounts, disconnect each one independently, or request complete account deletion from Settings. Signing out does not revoke connected YouTube authorizations. Some cleanup work may be retried if a provider is temporarily unavailable.

Data limitations

YouTube API data can be delayed, incomplete, unavailable, hidden, or rounded. Analytics dates and values may lag current activity. TubeMilestones records a milestone date only when it observes a tracked crossing; earlier achievements are labeled as before tracking. Older encrypted history can be temporarily unavailable while recent data remains visible.

TubeMilestones does not determine official YouTube Partner Program eligibility. Analytics watch time is not the qualified public watch-hours figure in YouTube Studio. User-entered values and threshold displays are guidance only. YouTube Studio and current policies remain authoritative for eligibility, regional availability, platform actions, and account decisions.
