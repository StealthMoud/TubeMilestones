Terms of use.

TubeMilestones is an early open-source utility. Use it as a personal progress companion, not as an official record or platform decision tool.

Independent application

TubeMilestones is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by YouTube or Google. YouTube, Google, and their product names are the property of their respective owners.

What the application provides

TubeMilestones reads authorized channel and Analytics data, stores a local history, and presents milestones, simple summaries, and user-created goals. It does not upload or manage YouTube content.

Data limitations

YouTube API data can be delayed, incomplete, unavailable, or rounded. Subscriber counts above certain levels are API-reported with limited precision. Analytics dates and values may lag behind current channel activity. TubeMilestones records a milestone date only when it observes a tracked crossing; earlier achievements are labeled as before tracking.

YouTube Partner Program guidance

TubeMilestones does not determine official YouTube Partner Program eligibility. Analytics watch time is not the same as the qualified public watch-hours figure in YouTube Studio. Manual values and threshold displays are guidance only. YouTube Studio and current YouTube policies remain authoritative for eligibility, regional availability, platform actions, and account decisions.
